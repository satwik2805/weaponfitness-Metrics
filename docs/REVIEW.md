# Weapon Fitness — Merged Critical Review & Punch List

_Lead-reviewer synthesis of 6 independent critical reviews (Member/Auth, Staff dashboards, Design-system/modals, Frontend architecture, Backend, Cross-cutting consistency). Deduplicated and prioritized P0 → P3._

---

## 1. State of the app (honest verdict)

**Yes — it is below the bar today, but the gap is unevenness, not rot.** All six reviewers independently converge on the same diagnosis: this reads as **two generations of code wearing one theme**. There is a genuinely excellent foundation — a disciplined token/type system, semantic color roles, well-built cinematic primitives (HeroBackdrop, Reveal, CountUp), clean and correctly-RLS'd dashboard services, a principled dual data plane, and a handful of near-flawless surfaces (the four role dashboards, Profile, Classes, Login). But that polish is **skin-deep and fragile**. The instant a member logs in, Home greets them with a Trifecta of four empty 0% rings that reads as a crashed dashboard; the instant anyone taps a button, they drop through a seam into the **old app** — 28-30 of ~32 modals are hand-rolled raw `<Modal>` shells that ignore the finished `Sheet`/`Input`/`Button` primitives, disagree with each other on animation and even hardcoded text color, and fail contrast (near-black text on red). The reason the polish is fragile is structural: **the core layout contract (cinematic hero + maxWidth 760 + padding + SectionTitle + skeleton + tab-bar clearance) was copy-pasted verbatim across 4–6 screens instead of extracted**, so it has already drifted (hero 336 vs 320 vs 300, name 44 vs 42) and the screens that didn't copy it silently drift on padding/width. There are a small number of true correctness/security defects — a native ring-centering bug (`inset:0`), a light-theme hero that fades into a hardcoded black band on a white page, and one backend router (`workout_log.py`) with **zero authorization on all 7 endpoints** (IDOR + XP fabrication). None of it is deep; almost every fix is mechanical extraction or a located one-line change. Fix the empty-state, lift the photography, extract one `DashboardScreen`/`CinematicHero` shell + `utils/format`, migrate the modals onto `Sheet`, and close the authz holes, and this jumps from "stitched together" to "one product." As shipped today, it is not at the Linear/Stripe/Vercel bar — and the hood (modals + duplicated screen scaffold) is exactly where it breaks.
